Data and calculation notes

This dashboard reads monetary columns from your CSV rather than replacing source amounts. It counts completed orders and treats refunded rows as negative adjustments if the file contains them. Amounts are summed as provided; missing monetary fields default to zero. In the generated 24-month CSV, Tax is 8.75% for SANFRANCISCO, 10% for SANJOSE, and 10.75% for OAKLAND or blank Place; Tip is 10% of Subtotal; Processing Fee is 3.75% of Subtotal + Tax + Tip. These are dataset assumptions, not verified local tax rates.

Year and Place filters apply to the KPIs, quarterly financials, segmentation, and export. For the year-over-year table, a selected Year is compared against its immediately preceding Year, while Place remains filtered. When All years is selected, the dashboard compares the latest year in the CSV against its prior year. Only quarters with data in either year appear; partial-year quarters may not be directly comparable.

CSV parsing supports quoted commas, escaped quotes, CRLF line endings, and parentheses for negative amounts.
